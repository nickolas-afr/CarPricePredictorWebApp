# User Instructions Manual - Car Price Predictor Web App

This manual is written for everyday users. You do **not** need technical knowledge to use the app.

If you want to add screenshots with arrows later, there are screenshot placeholders under each main step.

## 1. What this app does

This app helps you do two main things:

1. **Check whether a car price looks fair**
2. **Get car recommendations based on your needs**

It can also:

- switch between **light mode** and **dark mode**
- fill in some car details automatically if you have a **VIN number**

---

## 2. Before you start

Make sure:

- the web app is already open in your browser
- you have basic car information ready if you want to check a price
- you have the VIN number ready if you want to use the VIN decoder

---

## 3. Understanding the screen

When the app opens, you will see:

- **Home** in the left menu
- **Price Prediction** in the left menu
- **Find My Car** in the left menu
- a **theme toggle** at the top right area to switch light/dark mode

You can use the left menu at any time to move to another page.

## 4. How to check a car price

Use this feature when you already have a car in mind and want to know whether the asking price looks fair.

### Step 1 - Open the Price Prediction page

1. Click **Price Prediction** in the left menu.

**Screenshot placeholder:** Arrow pointing to the **Price Prediction** menu item.

### Step 2 - Optional: Use the VIN decoder

If you have the car's 17-character VIN:

1. Find the **Quick VIN Decoder** box at the top
2. Type the VIN into the box
3. Click **Decode VIN**
4. Wait for the success message

If the VIN is accepted, the app will automatically fill in some car details for you.

If you do **not** have a VIN, skip this part and fill in the form yourself.

**Important:** The VIN must have **17 characters** and must **not** contain the letters **I, O, or Q**.

**Screenshot placeholder:** VIN box and **Decode VIN** button with arrows.

### Step 3 - Fill in the car details

Complete the form on the left side of the page.

Fill in:

- **Brand (Make)** - for example BMW, Audi, Toyota
- **Model** - choose the model after choosing the brand
- **Production Year**
- **Mileage**
- **Fuel Type**
- **Transmission**
- **Horsepower**
- **Offer Type** - choose **Used** or **New**
- **Asking Price (€)** - the price the seller wants

Take your time. Move slowly through the form from top to bottom.

Fields marked with a red asterisk (**\***) are required.

**Screenshot placeholder:** Full form with arrows showing the order to complete it.

### Step 4 - Check the price

1. Click the **Check Price** button
2. Wait while the app analyzes the car

If you just want to see an example, click the **Test** button. This fills the form with sample car data automatically.

**Screenshot placeholder:** **Check Price** and **Test** buttons with arrows.

### Step 5 - Read the result

After the app finishes, the result appears on the right side.

You may see:

- **Good Price** - the asking price looks fair
- **Too High** - the price looks higher than expected
- **Too Low** - the price looks lower than expected

You will also see:

- a **deal score gauge**
- **your price**
- the **predicted price**
- the **difference**
- the **typical price range**

### How to understand the deal score

- **Green** = good deal
- **Yellow** = fair or slightly high
- **Red** = expensive or overpriced

**Screenshot placeholder:** Example result area with arrows pointing to the status, gauge, and price table.

## 5. How to find recommended cars

Use this feature when you do **not** know which car to buy and want the app to suggest some options.

### Step 1 - Open the Find My Car page

1. Click **Find My Car** in the left menu

**Screenshot placeholder:** Arrow pointing to **Find My Car** in the menu.

### Step 2 - Complete Step 1: Budget & Condition

Enter your basic limits:

- **Max Budget**
- **Minimum Year**
- **Condition** - Used, New, or Any
- **Max Mileage** if you are not choosing only new cars

When finished, click **Next →**

**Screenshot placeholder:** Step 1 page with arrows to budget, year, condition, mileage, and **Next**.

### Step 3 - Complete Step 2: Powertrain

Choose the driving preferences you want:

- one or more **Fuel Type** choices
- **Transmission**
- **Min HP**
- **Max HP**

Then click **Next →**

If you made a mistake, click **← Back**.

**Screenshot placeholder:** Step 2 page with arrows to fuel type, transmission, HP fields, and **Next**.

### Step 4 - Complete Step 3: Body & Comfort

Choose:

- **Body Style**
- **Minimum Seats**

Then click **Get Recommendations**.

**Note:** Body style and seat count are used as helpful hints for recommendations.

**Screenshot placeholder:** Step 3 page with arrows to body style, seats, and **Get Recommendations**.

### Step 5 - Read your recommendations

The app will show one or more car suggestions.

For each recommendation, you may see:

- the **make and model**
- a short explanation
- the **predicted fair price**
- the **typical price range**
- a **deal score**

You can also click **Check this car's price** to go to the price checker page.

If you want to begin again, click **Start Over**.

**Screenshot placeholder:** Recommendation results with arrows to the explanation, price range, deal score, and buttons.

## 6. How to switch light mode and dark mode

If the screen looks too bright or too dark:

1. Find the small theme switch at the top of the page
2. Click it once to change the theme
3. Click again if you want to switch back

The app should remember your choice in your browser.

**Screenshot placeholder:** Theme toggle with an arrow.

## 7. Simple tips for first-time users

- Start with **Price Prediction** if you already found a car online
- Start with **Find My Car** if you are still deciding what to buy
- Use the **Test** button if you want to see how the app works before entering real data
- If a dropdown list is long, scroll slowly until you find what you need
- If something looks wrong, go back and check your numbers carefully

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

### I clicked Check Price but I see "Model Not Available"

This usually means the app has not been fully set up yet. It is **not your fault as a user**.

Ask the person who manages the app to check the machine learning model file.

### My VIN is not accepted

Check that:

- the VIN has exactly **17 characters**
- it does not include **I**, **O**, or **Q**
- you typed it correctly

### I did not get any car recommendations

Try again with wider choices, for example:

- increase your budget
- lower the minimum year
- allow more fuel types
- widen the horsepower range

### The page looks too dark or too bright

Use the theme switch at the top of the page to change the appearance.

---

## 9. Quick summary

If you only remember four things, remember these:

1. Use **Price Prediction** to check whether a specific car price looks fair
2. Use **Find My Car** to get suggestions based on your needs
3. Use the **VIN decoder** only if you already have a valid VIN number
4. Use the **theme toggle** at the top if you want light mode or dark mode
